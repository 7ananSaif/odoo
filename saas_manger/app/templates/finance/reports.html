Definitions

MRR
Sum of active subscriptions normalised to one month (yearly plans divided by 12), after discounts.
Outstanding
Unpaid balances on sent and overdue invoices.
Overdue
Unpaid balances on invoices past their due date.
Yearly revenue
Total of all non-cancelled invoices issued in the current year.
Churn rate
Churned tenants divided by active + churned tenants, over the window.
